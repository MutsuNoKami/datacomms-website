Our Story

Who We Are

Jr Data Comms provides data cabling installation services for commercial clients. We work on structured cabling, copper and fibre optic installations, network cabinet fit-outs and related network infrastructure.

We work with businesses of various sizes — from single-office fit-outs to multi-site projects. Each project is approached in the same way: understand the requirement, plan the work, and carry it out with care.

We hold public liability insurance and aim to keep up to date with current industry practices so that our work reflects recognised installation methods.
